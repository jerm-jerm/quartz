## Events
Cassius cast *Dream* on Benzosia after hearing her name again. In the dream she is sitting on a throne of ice and seems uninterested in Cassius. He tells her that he does not want to make new enemies and she tells him that she has no idea who he is. He notes an insignia on her rob of a downward pointed serpent with ripped off arms, before leaving saying that he got what he wanted.

In the morning Otiluke and Allara teleport into the igloo. He states they are coming from The Nine Hells, but he hates the cold so he leaves.

Varrick ask Gin Bon 4 if he is able to fight. He says he comes from a peaceful village but when Varrick ask him to demonstrate 5/10ths of his skill he appears very proficient. His eyes also become more red and his responses seem terse.

Back on the path of the barbarians and the stolen goods, the crew run into an old monk-like man who calls himself Sora. Gin Bon 4 seems to think this man in powerful, and Sora indicates he is looking for a fight. Despite some goading words, the group reminds Varrick that they have no reason to fight this man and they move on. Further down the path they encounter a frigid spire with odd footprints of various sizes and small charms at the entrance. Upon entering Gin Bon 4 notices an unusual type of magic within the cavern and calls it Rhinethorn. A group of kobolds then descend on them and seem angry about their presence. Unable to communicate to defuse the situation, they begin to battle, killing a number of Kobolds. Before further bloodshed, Allara ask The [[Raven Queen]] for divine intervention. Taking on the visage of the Raven Queen herself, Allara commands the Kobolds to flee and commands an ancient white dragon to reveal itself. Although she attempts to get it to flee the area, it is able to resist the command and appears ready to battle the group.
