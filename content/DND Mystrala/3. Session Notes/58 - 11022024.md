## Events
Cassius wakes up the from his dream where he talked with [[Alexi Faraday]] who requested the [[Pieces of The Inevitable]], he begins stress preparing for danger. Dunsparrow comes into Cas's room and ask him if he has her back and he says, "Of course, without a doubt."

Cas, Sundara, Samugi, and Cobb meet up for breakfast. Cas tells them how he thinks he pissed off Faraday and thinks that Faraday is sending something or someone to get the pieces. Cas obviously appears stressed and on edge and wants to come up with a plant. They invite [[Ottoluke Organdorf]] over to breakfast to ask him questions about the Pieces. He consideres that maybe a Terrasque could digest and disenchant an item. 

They decide to go shopping for magical components in New Calim with Ottoluke. They make it to the Mortal Promise where Cassius reunites with his family. He has a lute contest with his father who tells him that [[Chalice Manath]] was eyeing his girlfriend again. Cas confronts Chalice about this, thinking that Chalice was going after Dunsparrow. She confirms that she has absolutely no interest in her and has only stolen his girlfriend one time.

Cas and Sundara visit [[Solonor's Workshop]]. As they enter the courtyard, they see [[The Engineer]], Anastasia leaving. Sundara greets her, while Anastasia glares at her clearly upset. Anastasia mentions her disdain for Sundara's perceived laissez-faire attitude regarding Solonor's work and her ability to come and go as Sundara pleases.  As Sundara uses the workshop clockwork key to get into the workshop. While there, they witness clockwork devices floating in the workshop, apparently made previous apprentices. Including a beautiful clockwork xylophone/harp created by someone named Aelion (this name was erased from Cas' memory without his knowledge). 

Cas, Sundara, and Solonor sit down and discuss their reason for visiting. They discuss if destroying the [[Pieces of The Inevitable]] would be possible. Solonor offers instead to hold the Pieces and use the to eventually recreate The Inevitable. Solonor thinks that if he reanimates The Inevitable it will defend the heart of [[Karsus]]. Cassius sends a subtle spell message to Sundara stating that this might be a good idea, out loud Sundara says, "I trust him. I do." 

Cas steps away to go to the library. Sundara and Solonor discuss his 'failsafe' for his 'game' with Faraday. He shows her a new [[Clockwork Devices|clockwork sentinel]] that is able to use some sort of antimagic energy to fight a displacer beast. While in this cavern Sundara confronts Solonor and ask him about his previous apprentices and if he has been in relationships with other apprentices and he says it has only happened once. Solonor ask if Sundara wants him to get rid of Anastasia. Sundara tells him how she will not share him and he ensures her that he loves Sundara and is willing to get rid of Anastasia if Sundara wants him to. 

Solonor and Sundara share a kiss. She asks if she is delaying his plans while she travels around with The Remnants. Their foreheads touch as he tells her that he was initially obsessive and pushy about her magical development and that he didn't need to be because she developed into a powerful sorcerer in her own way. He tells her he isn't worried about what she told him regarding [[Phaerimm]], because he knew that he wouldn't let that happen to her. Sundara hears his voice echoing in her head and notices he is casting a high level spell. As Solonor collapses in her arms she makes out the final words of his incantation, "I wish."
