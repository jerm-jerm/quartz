## Events
After casting *Protection from Good and Evil*, the [[Oni]] is out of [[Thaunia]]. The group keeps her locked in the Brigg for 24-36 hours, while being watched around the clock. They subsequently let her out and after asking if they wanted her to stick around, the group hesitated, and she left. She disappears into the crowd of the city.

[[Allara]] heads to the [[Gilded Glave]] to return the few remaining goods left from the transport to [[Alhadria]]. Most of the cargo, along with her previous crew, had been lost in an attack. The [[Gilded Glave]] offers the group a few job opportunities.
	- Investigate criminal activity around Reggie's shop 
	- Hunt down unkillable chimera's that killed four memebers of Bobby the Gent's team 
	- Locate Billy Barns a missing smuggler

The group approaches Bobby the Gent. His organization, the Creature Company, hunts creatures, salvaging their remains to be sent to the Soulshard Apothecary for the [[Voidwing Sisterhood]] to transform into potions.

The party then trekked to the Snow Capped Mountains to the north of [[New Calim]]. Using a clockwork trap created by Samugi, they successfully kill two chimeras. Afterwards, a druid appeared in their midst.
