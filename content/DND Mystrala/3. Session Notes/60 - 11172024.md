## Events
Cassius teleports back into Barrowmoor Manor just as a powerful spell finishes, shutting down the teleportation sigil and blocking all forms of planar travel. He urgently tells the group that Elhorn is approaching with his entire faction. The group gathers everyone on the island to prepare for the upcoming attack.

Reaching out to [[Leland]], they learn that [[Solonor]] stopped at [[The Icebreaker]] and sent it to their location. Allara sends her familiar to scout the skies and tells everyone to go outside. Once outside they see meteors streaking across the sky, targeting various critical locations on [[Karsus]]—places Sundara knows to be important to Solonor, including his [[Solonor's Workshop|workshop]], [[Burning Hands]], Serrick’s Run, and an area outside of [[New Calim]]. 

[[Sarah Stoneheart]] arrives unexpectedly. She kisses Varrick and explains that she was praying to Helm and received guidance to come here. Samugi tinkers with clockwork sentinels, modifying them to carry dragon poison bombs, and equips his astral creature with a dark iron cannon.

Sundara calls Anastasia to warn her about the incoming meteors and urges her to protect Solonor. A massive portal opens up above Barrowmoor and a ship equipped with Clockwork Sentinels comes through. Anastasia looks somberly at Sundara and tells her that Solonor sent her away and plans to face the oncoming threat alone.

Varrick contacts Heskell, asking him to fight with them or take out Elhorn’s clones. Heskell’s response is cryptic and unhelpful at first, though he later arrives disguised as a younger version of Cobb. The group also reaches out to Ollie Silver of the Rust Alley Whalers, who agrees to help, though he demands payment if they all survive.

As the group finishes their preparations and the fleet closes in, they discover one of the clockwork servants disassembled, its poison bomb missing. Just as [[Heskell]] casts a spell to gain insight into the situation, the manor is struck by artillery fire from the approaching ships.

The battle begins in earnest as Sarah Stoneheart transforms, revealing herself to be Valesca Vale. But the group has no time to react as Elhorn arrives, commanding a mix of [[oni]] and giants. Among them is a familiar face—[[Thaunia]], now twisted and almost unrecognizable, her demonic possession more pronounced than ever. The Remnants, along with Otiluke, Cobb, and Heskell, engage in a fight against Elhorn's forces. Above, the Scourge Angels arrive in the skies, joining Anastasia’s forces and [[Reyna]]'s ship to battle Elhorn’s fleet. Together, they slow the amount of enemies approaching Barrowmoor.

Varrick confronts Elhorn directly, stabbing him in the chest and declaring this as his revenge for the death of his son. Elhorn responds coldly, claiming he doesn’t even know who Varrick is. Heskell confirms that, upon Elhorn’s death, his consciousness immediately transfers to a clone stored in a secure location called, The Vault.
