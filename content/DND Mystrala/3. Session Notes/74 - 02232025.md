## Events
The Remnants face off against the ancient white dragon, Eldithar, in a brutal battle. Eldithar soars through the cavern, striking with its tail, biting and dragging its victims, and unleashing devastating blasts of ice breath.

Varrick pursues the dragon relentlessly, calling on Sundara to teleport him using _Vortex Warp_ while she hurls motes of starlight at both the dragon and its kobold worshipers. Cassius hits Eldithar with cursed strikes, that results in each hit landing harder. Shar moves in and out of the fight, landing sneak attacks before vanishing into extradimensional spaces. A mysterious archer also aids them, striking at the dragon and its followers. Allara tries to keep up healing the massive amounts of damage most everyone is taking.

By the end of the battle, Cassius—barely holding himself together from the damage he sustained—has a tense exchange with Varrick before abruptly teleporting away. Sundara follows him, and they find the dragon’s hoard. Sundara says she has noticed a change in him during battle and Cas wonders if its the Charm of the Zealot or other magic items he might be using. He has a new lust for violence especially when he goes unconscious. He reassures her he wouldn't do anything to hurt the group.

Among the treasures are two of the three paintings they were tasked with retrieving, along with several other valuable items. One additional painting catches Sundara’s attention despite its unsettling aura.

The archer introduces himself as Coren, a goliath teenager from Clan Serpa. He explains to Varrick that Eldithar had been terrorizing his village, and he had planned to kill it alone. Cassius calls his plan reckless, while Varrick, intrigued, asks if he can visit Coren’s clan with his wife and daughter, hoping to reconnect with their giant heritage. Initially dismissive, Coren calls Varrick a “southerner” and seems uninterested in helping. However, after dividing the spoils of battle and acknowledging the Remnants' prowess, he concedes they have at least earned a meeting with his clan’s elders.

Shar gathers additional loot, while Sundara collects Rhinethorn, which Gin Bon 4 identifies as elemental energy from the eyes of Karsus. With their mission nearly complete, the group decides to return to their contact to free Samugi as soon as possible, planning to visit the goliath or warforged village some other time.
