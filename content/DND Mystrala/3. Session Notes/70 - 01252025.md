## Events
The battle begins as Captain Oliver Bentley leads his ship, The Leviathan’s Grasp, against [[The Icebreaker]]. Leland recognizes the vessel—he once crewed it. The fight is intense, with the Remnants and their allies battling fiercely to hold their ground. Various crew members and friends step in to assist, including Leland, Nick, Dunsparrow Beaumont, Klagger, and Briggs. In a critical moment, Danny takes a devastating blow while protecting one of the captains—either Cassius or Sundara.

Amid the chaos, a dying sea elf whispers, "It’s okay, you’ll soon be swallowed by the Fifth Tide."

As Bentley confronts Cassius with fury, accusing the Remnants of leaving him for dead. Cassius, unshaken, replies, "I truly don’t even know who you are." The words rattle Bentley. When the Remnants strike him down, he gives Cassius a chilling look—one not of fear, but of knowing. There’s a sense that Bentley was involved in something deeper, something connected to the primordial being [[Dagon]] and possibly the World Tide.
