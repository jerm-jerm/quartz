## History
Known for her sharp intellect and cunning strategies. [[Dunsparrow Beaumont|Dunsparrow]] describes her as an immensely powerful witch and the world’s foremost expert on demonology. [[Chalice Manath]] uncovered a conspiracy linking the Queen to Duke [[Oswald Beaumont]], whom she allegedly used to infiltrate governmental systems. These efforts, combined with [[Solonor|Solonor's]] [[Clockwork Devices]], appear aimed at destabilizing governments to extend her dominion. Public image is largely positive.

### Current Activities
Her broader agenda remains elusive, her leadership is often lauded for its effectiveness, with many citing her as a visionary ruler who "stuck it to the FETC." There are recurring appearances by a mysterious gentleman suitor. This suitor takes on various forms and names, sparking persistent rumors of the Queen’s involvement with a demon prince (?possibly Orcus, the only known demon prince to the group, or maybe Levistus, the Devil Prince). Very toxic relationship with both of them doing their worst to each other in a cycle. 

### Personality Traits
- Celebrated by the public for her brilliance and intelligence
- Compassionate but calculating

### Relation to The Remnants 
- She is aligned with Duke Oswald Beaumont and is providing support in his take over of [[New Calim]]
