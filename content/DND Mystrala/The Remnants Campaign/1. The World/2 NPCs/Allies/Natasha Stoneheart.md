## History
Natasha Stoneheart was raised by her mother, Sarah Stoneheart, in relative isolation for her early life, they moved to New Calim after their home was destroyed, likely by The [[Circle of Ruin and Thistle]]. She knows she had an older brother who passed away but only in the last year learned the identity of her father, Varrick. 

Since meeting The Remnants, they have learned of her defiance and risk taking, such as when she snuck aboard [[The Icebreaker]] and stabbed the ship's chef, [[Bordon Ramley]]. She has a stuffed minotaur toy, which the Bone King once stole and used to threaten her safety, sending it to Varrick as proof of his ability to reach her. Natasha has also been blackmailing Barrel Back Bill, coercing him into silence about his backroom dealings and illicit sales behind Sarah’s back. Her cunning manipulation has left Bill deeply afraid of her. Varrick, upon learning of the situation, gave [[Barrel Back Bill]] one month to confess his actions to Sarah.

### Current Activities
During the recent battle for the The [[Mortal Promise]], she took on the responsibility of safeguarding younger children.
