## History
Langston is an area of general 'lawlessness' run by The Bone King. The area is seen as a necessary evil and is often overlooked by governmental heads. Home of the production of [[Spark|tincture]].

Elhorn has destroyed all but one other factions hold on Kraken hunting. Elhorn ruled over Langston in a ruthless, unpredictable fashion. He was publicly killed by The Remnants on Barrowmoor Island. Despite having numerous clones, his grip on the city slipped and Langston fell into more chaos. The group suspects that Heskell will take over The Bone Kings mantle.
