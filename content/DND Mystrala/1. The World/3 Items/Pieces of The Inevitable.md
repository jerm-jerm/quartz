Also known as Vestiges of the Moon King. Created by [[The Savage Seven]] after killing [[Aurunat, The Inevitable]] 

## Gavel of the Judge 
Massive pure white battle hammer, overall impractical for use in it's design. Was the weapon of The Inevitable. Currently sitting in [[The Shattered Spire]] in [[Karsus Folly]] inside the ?Chamber of Dreamers as part of Prince Alexi Faraday's magical collection. Otiluke notes that it makes cool sounds in use and is impossibly light. It would essentially dissolve enemies into pink bloody mist. [[Conrad Gricks]] has been seen wielding it. Not technically a Piece of the Inevitable, was actually its weapon.

## The Harmonic Resonator
Originally one of Duke Oswald Beaumont's weapons. Was taken by the group after killing him outside of the Whispering Tower in New Calim. Identifying this object allowed the group to find out more of the history regarding [[The Savage Seven]]. The group held on to this object until willingly giving it to Faraday in exchange for [[Varrick]]'s resurrection. 

## The Pelt of the Moon Warden
A pale, white-ish stone-like armor that was being worn by Gunner. Was created from the skin of Aurunat. 

## Legend of the Moonwarden/Ledger of the Fated Moon
Otiluke gambled away his piece of The Inevitable for drinking money years ago while in Langston. Allows the user to select from various magical charms and have them switch and according to the user or the books wishes. Was taken by The Remnants upon the death of the 'original' Elhorn. In that same day [[Sundara]] reluctantly traded the book to Alexi Faraday in exchange for Solonor's life. 

## Silks of the Moonwarden
Obtained from [[Ironsoul]] after a battle to prove themselves.

## Heart of the Fated  Moon
A melon sized ruby colored gem. Sundara learned from [[Elowen Elisthorn]] and Faraday that her brother Nelaros had become a part of the gem while he was an apprentice under Solonor. Solonor say Nelaros cast a spell/ritual that was meant for himself.
