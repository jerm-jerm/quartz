## History
Sundara's cousin on her mother's side, who left Valendri to work with Solonor after undergoing the Trial of Future Visions. 

Sundara and her initially crossed paths while in [[New Calim]] during Duke [[Oswald Beaumont]]'s first take over. Anastasia was seen being called to by The [[Clockwork Devices|Clockwork Sentinels]] that needed repair, and was referred to as The Engineer. 

She and The Remnants met face-to-face for the first time at the Last Will and Testament of [[Gunner Ironford]]. She presented as a proxy for Solonor. And despite trying to leave, she was cornered by Sundara. Anastasia made it clear that she was opposed to Sundara's dismissal of Solonor's plan. 

After Sundara and Solonor reconciled, Sundara made a visit to Solonor’s Workshop, Anastasia was dismissed by Solonor shortly before Sundara's arrival. Their encounter was tense; Anastasia, clearly upset, implied resentment toward Sundara for her perceived lack of commitment to Solonor's cause. Sundara later asked Solonor if he had been romantically involved with Anastasia, which he denied, offering to remove Anastasia if Sundara wished.

Before the Battle of Barrowmoor, Anastasia worked to stabilize Solonor’s workshop after his magic was weakened by casting *Wish*. When Sundara warned her of an incoming attack from Faraday’s Meteor Swarm, Anastasia was reluctantly sent to Barrowmoor to help The Remnants, appearing somber and deeply affected by Solonor's decision to face the battle alone. During the battle she suffered the loss of her right arm. 

### Current Activities
After the Battle of Barrowmoor, Anastasia apologized for rejecting Sundara’s earlier attempts at kindness, and Sundara offered Allara’s healing services to regenerate her lost arm. Anastasia accepted this offer and shared her plans to rebuild Solonor’s workshop before continuing her studies in Karsus Folly.
