## History
Dunsparrow Beaumont was adopted by [[Oswald Beaumont]] as a young child. She met  [[Cassius|Cassius Manath]] at some point and became entangled in a romantic relationship that was frowned upon by her father and ultimately resulted in Cassius being thrown in [[Ironford Prison]]. Subsequently, tensions in their relationship arose after she witnessed The Remnants kill her father ([[21 - 02102024|Session 21]]) before she had even found out Cassius was out of prison. 

Despite this, Dunsparrow later helped Cassius and The Remnants avoid murder charges for The Duke's death. She feigned that Oswald was severely ill and orchestrated a legal process to inherit the Beaumont estate. When The Remnants later rescued [[Chalice Manath]], Chalice revealed that she had been a prisoner of the Beaumont's for months—first held by Oswald, then by Dunsparrow—before being handed over to [[Gunner Ironford]]. Chalice speculated that Dunsparrow handed her over to gather information without directly conducting the interrogation herself.

Dunsparrow admitted to preventing Oswald's resurrection to protect Cassius, believing that her actions made her ultimately responsible for her father’s death. Though she grew in influence in [[New Calim]], she was never officially recognized as its ruler. Members of the [[Sea of Swords]], particularly [[Sarah Stoneheart]], noted that the city was in the process of granting Dunsparrow unilateral decision-making power (Sessions [[34 - 05122024|34]] and [[37 - 06012024|37]]) but criticized her decisions as harmful to the people.

She was later seen speaking with [[Serpa]] at [[Alexi Faraday|Alexi Faraday's]] charity ball and was identified as having markings of a member of The [[Circle of Ruin and Thistle]] on her hands. Dunsparrow later confirmed her membership in the Circle, implying it was a pursuit of power. She revealed that she was under the thumb of the [[Queen of Brightwater]], expressing stress and pressure to maintain her position and authority in New Calim.

Dunsparrow admitted her dislike for her father and inviting Cassius to return to New Calim with her, assuring him he would always have a place there ([[30 - 04142024|Session 30]]). Later, she invited The Remnants to stay with her at Barrowmoor Manor outside of Langston, where she introduced them to [[Heskell]], warning them not to make him an enemy.

After learning of [[The Bone King|Elhorn's]] plans to attack Barrowmoor Manor, Dunsparrow attempted to kidnap Cassius from The Remnants to ensure his safety, which resulted in an emotionally charged confrontation between the two.

### Current Activities
Following her actions before The Battle of Barrowmoor, Dunsparrow was imprisoned by the newly resurrected Duke Oswald Beaumont in a magical prison beneath The Whispering Tower. She was later rescued by Cassius, after which she declared that Oswald was no longer her father.

### Personality Traits
- Level-headed and maintains her composure even when angry
- Can come across as cold and stoic even to people she cares about
- Willing to make difficult choices to protect those she cares about, even if it affects their view of her

### Relation to The Remnants 
- Romantically tied to [[Cassius]] Manath, they reconciled their differing opinions about Cas's role in the death of her father and Dunsparrow's role in the kidnapping of his sister
- [[Varrick]] killed her father; she calls Varrick a vagabond and murderer and appears surprised with how important he is to Cassius
