[[Sundara]]'s cousin on her mother's side. Was also influenced to leave [[Valendri]] to work with [[Solonor]] after her Trial of Future Visions. Is upset with how much Sundara doesn't seem to want to work with Solonor.

As they enter the courtyard, they see [[The Engineer]], Anastasia leaving. Sundara greets her, while Anastasia glares at her clearly upset. Anastasia mentions her disdain for Sundara's perceived laissez-faire attitude regarding Solonor's work and her ability to come and go as Sundara pleases.
