## Impression
Celeste has always carried herself quieter. Steady, observant, and unhurried. She was spared the intensity of my upbringing, though she seems convinced I overromanticize her childhood. Perhaps she is right. I can see the strain of the new expectations on her, even if she hides it well. She is capable and applies herself to a role she was pressed into, though I once wished she could have lived a life not shaped by duty.

## Known Record
Third child of [[Admiral Thessa Katharis|Thessa]] and Dainric Katharis; second daughter. Trained in bladesong and naval tactics, though under a regimen less severe than [[Luminora Katharis|mine]] until my removal from succession. Now heir apparent and bearer of the Meridian Edge. Assigned Vaelin as her swornblade.

She always favored the library over the training halls. Her first War Games ended in a loss to House Lysandra.
