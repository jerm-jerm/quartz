## Impression
Her presence felt like facing not a being but an enduring principle. All her descendants carry pieces of her celestial origin; some lean into it, but House Katharis has always regarded it as a liability, a lapse of control. Time does not touch her as it touches us. When I stood before her, radiant tears marked her face, and I could not tell whether they had fallen a moment earlier or had been waiting there for centuries.

## Known Record
Xalithea is known as the prophet who absorbed nations into the Founder’s dominion. Celestial heritage is known, though I only recently learned its full nature. That she is a [[The Scourge|scourge angel]], meant to erase civilization for renewal.

Though Xalithea The Forever Blade remains largely in a state of stasis, she still observes her daughters and feels the extinguishing of each descendant’s life as it occurs. She meets us only at turning points, [[Admiral Thessa Katharis|Mother]] after Karsus, and now myself, yet she offers neither commands nor counsel, merely her presence and her blessing.
