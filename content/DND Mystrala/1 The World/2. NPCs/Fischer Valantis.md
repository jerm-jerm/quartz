## Impression
Fischer Valantis seems unable to imagine a world that would not applaud him. I have yet to determine if he understands the difference between spectacle and strategy. He possesses a natural charisma that compels others to fall in step behind him. For a son of Conscriptus, he shines brightly, perhaps too brightly, accustomed to the attention of crowds and the certainty that his voice will be heard.

## Known Record
Son of House Valantis, the foremost militaristic House of Conscriptus. He was honorably discharged from the Blackwater Armada upon selection to the Ledgermen service. An obvious contrast to the uproar my selection brought. A testament to the value of daughters over sons. Participated in the War Games some years prior where he engaged in a duel against Vaelin Katharis that ended officially in a draw.
