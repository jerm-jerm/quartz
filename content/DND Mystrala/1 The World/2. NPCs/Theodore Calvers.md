## Impression
Theodore Bishop is awkward, rambling, and more animated than I expected a bishop to be. At first, I mistook it for eccentricity, but I think it is nerves. He’s earnest and it spills out of him unchecked. Still, he is pious and competent enough to keep his post.

## Known Record
Recently appointed Bishop of Vellhum Heights, following the death of his predecessor. Comes from a long line of clergy. Does not appear to have any formal training in arcana or combat.

Assigned to chaperone our investigation in the Sanctiforge, but our presence dragged him into danger. He was kidnapped from jail and nearly transformed into a mindflayer. I find myself irritated that Charles’ recklessness nearly cost Calvers so much, from what I can tell about him, he does not deserve that.

He conducted the burial rites for [[Yedan-Neric]] and commented on how Yedan was the first face he saw during his rescue.
