_Designation: Psychic Aberrants_  
_Status: Active Threat_

Mindflayers are not the mythic terrors of ancient heresies. They are psychic aberrants, parasitic creatures operating as a collective will, not individuals. The tadpoles are altered and smuggled in. Infected with vampirism. Capable now of sustaining themselves on blood. Their hosts grow wild and feral, burning in the sunlight.

In the Shardlight District, the water is tainted. People wander the streets wasting away, but the deaths don’t align solely with deprivation. A magical resonance lingers in the body, [[Mazarin]] describes it as a vast interconnected room.

The Ordinance Precinct in the Shardlight were creating mindflayers. I say it plainly because there is no other word. I saw what they nearly did to Theodore Calvers. We were close to failure. If we had waited even a moment longer, he would have been consumed, transformed.

I do not yet understand the scale of their presence.
