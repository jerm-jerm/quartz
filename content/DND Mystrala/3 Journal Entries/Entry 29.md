The final qualifying rounds prior to the principal duels were conducted in a reduced bracket—twenty contenders, six forfeitures. The remaining engagements proceeded without delay. I fought each in sequence.

There is a rhythm to consecutive combat that differs from isolated duels. Fatigue accumulates not only in the body, but in the precision of thought. I accounted for this. Opponents varied in discipline and style; one, an orc of considerable strength, favored overwhelming force over measured technique. Predictable. He fell accordingly.

During one exchange, I executed a Xeresian guard—clean, exact, and without hesitation. The reaction from the crowd was immediate and divided. Some recognized the form for what it represents: lineage, training, and implication. Others responded with visible disdain. The technique is not widely practiced outside of imperial circles. Its use here, even veiled beneath disguise, was… noted.

That may have been an error.

Or it may have been necessary.

By the conclusion of the bracket, attention had shifted. Not fully—my identity remains intact—but enough to suggest that certain Houses are no longer observing us as curiosities. They are evaluating.

Following the matches, House Maelthrumm delivered a collection of rewards. The presentation was understated, but deliberate. Among the items was a bow of clear enchantment—well-balanced, responsive, and crafted with intent beyond ornamentation. I accepted it as one accepts all such gifts: with awareness of both its utility and its implication. Nothing given in Conscriptus is without expectation.

**NOTE:** Kessaria approached the group shortly thereafter, delivering unspecified items before departing. Enoch accompanied her. Observations of their interaction suggest familiarity not yet disclosed. The nature of their connection remains unclear.

**NOTE:** Charles reports an encounter with an individual identifying himself as Gracious—an imposing figure, approximately nine feet in height, bearing six fingers and presenting traits consistent with Karsus lineage. He claimed affiliation with House Lysandra under the pretense of diplomatic outreach from Karsus Folly. Subsequent interaction between Charles and this individual extended beyond initial contact. By morning, Gracious had departed without trace. Charles experienced a sudden and overwhelming compulsion to locate his wife, accompanied by physiological distress.

I encountered Charles and this individual briefly alongside Marrion. The interaction was… unremarkable on the surface. In retrospect, that may be the most concerning detail.

The compulsion was identified as arcane in origin—structured, invasive, and not self-generated. Marrion facilitated its removal with efficiency during the morning interval. The process was controlled, though not without strain. Charles’ condition stabilized following intervention.

We conducted this assessment over breakfast.

Sal prepared coffee. It was, as expected, volatile.

The timing of this intrusion is not coincidental. Influence exerted at the threshold of the final duels suggests intent to destabilize before critical engagements. Whether Gracious acted independently or as an extension of a larger design remains unknown.

What is clear is this: observation has shifted into interference.

We proceed regardless.

The final duels remain.
