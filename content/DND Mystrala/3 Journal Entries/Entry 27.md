We were intercepted at the Blackwater Strait before making port. The attackers descended from dragonback, clad in deliberately obscured armor, and claimed authority beyond that of the Empire. Their demand was immediate submission. We declined.

The engagement was direct and we persevered. Their remains burned to ash shortly after defeat, suggesting either a failsafe enchantment or an intentional erasure of origin. This was not a disorganized force.

In the aftermath, we withdrew to the captain’s quarters to assess. In peeling the many layers of pain from the armor that remained, partial heraldry suggested a connection to Brightwater. The attackers referenced a queen. Whether this referred to Brightwater’s ruling power or another claimant remains uncertain.

I conducted a divination to clarify the origin of the command. The response was neither ambiguous nor fully explanatory. The voice was Xalithea’s. The instruction was simple: trust my instincts. I have recorded the result accordingly. For an unknown reason the [[Queen of Brightwater]] is interested in my demise.

We arrived in Conscriptus without further interruption. The city is unchanged in structure but heightened in atmosphere as the War Games begin. We made our way to The Crucible of Five Crowns where it is hosted. We entered under disguise to avoid unnecessary scrutiny. Within the exhibition halls, the relic blades were on display. The Meridian Edge was present. Its internal constellations remain fascinating, precise and beyond replication. It is not merely a weapon. It is a statement of origin. I miss wielding it. 

Other artifacts were displayed alongside it: Vorynth’s veiled blade, Lysandra’s armament, and the Sovereign Blade. Each carries weight, though none mirror the same balance of elegance and inevitability. I observed. I did not linger. ***

Registration for the Games was formalized shortly thereafter. The structure remains consistent with prior years: multiple trials to determine seeding, followed by a final duel among selected champions. Mortality is acknowledged as a possibility, not an exception. We assigned roles with efficiency. Sal to the magical duel. Enoch to the obstacle course. Charles to marksmanship. Mazarin to the diplomatic gala. I will take the final duels.

Sal entered the Proving Pit and was nearly eliminated in the opening exchange, struck by a concentrated lightning assault. He endured. Whether by resilience, intervention, or refusal to yield is unclear. He adapted mid-engagement, shifted tactics, and closed distance with precision. The outcomem, a draw for first place, was met with both confusion and acclaim.

**NOTE:** Records and private correspondence indicate that Charles Brand has continued communication with Belladonna Vorynth. During these exchanges, the name “Orcus,” identified as a Demon Prince, was referenced in relation to his weapon. The extent of this influence remains under observation.

Performance will determine positioning. Positioning will determine outcome.
