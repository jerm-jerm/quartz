Horus arrived with the resupply. Though he expressed approval of our progress, he issued a clear directive: we have one week to identify and resolve the root cause before another Ledgermen crew is dispatched to finish the assignment. He believes the Temple of Aqua Vitae, connected directly to the contaminated wells, is the source.

 *NOTE: Horus' resupply log indicates Charles questioned Horus about the 181st Steel Covenant unit. Horus expressed uncertainty any survived. He emphasized to Charles Brand that this and future operations should be handled quietly, a contrast to the doctrine he was once trained under.*

We began reconnaissance of the temple. Mazarin and Yedan disguised themselves and molded into the crowd; Charles and I took an elevated position atop an abandoned apartment building. Charles has acquired a magical construct purpose built to fabricate ammunition, a thing he insists on calling Mr. Chompy. I do not understand naming a tool. Still, output appears functional and it has an obvious tactical value. 

While monitoring the area, we noted a disproportionate Ordinance presence: six patrols within a single alley’s distance, all positioned to avoid the temple itself. Then, Elvire appeared, flanked by two armored attendants. He wore modified Steel Covenant shock armor and carried a greatsword. The crowds parted around him, reverence bordering on cultic response.

While on watch, I asked Charles to examine my wound. He carries field supplies, and I know little of proper healing. He asked if I’d ever been injured before. The question nearly made me laugh. Of course I have. I thought to tell him about some of my more severe injuries but thought better of it. I did admit that serious injury in my House was handled by... replacement. Saying it aloud felt strange. Not wrong, but exposed. Embarrassing, even. I dismissed it quickly and instead asked about his family and childhood. He doesn’t remember it.  

I recalled what I had seen in his mind when I cast detect magic, evidence of  Class-4 memory alignment, layered remediation. I decided not to ask further. A kind of Pruning in its own right, and not a gentle one. If given the choice I would take my family's blade over was done to him.

Mazarin and Yedan-Neric returned,. White-robed clerics, Water Saints, enter to fill their vessels, while reciting scripture from the _Manual of Ascension_. The temple though is actually public. Yedan overheard mention of “divine hydraulics” and a figure named Mother Valerie in the lower levels. He also saw an individual disappear into a crypt in the graveyard behind the temple.

A sudden pulse from underground swept through us. For an instant, all of us felt suddenly observed.

We shifted our focus to the graveyard. No activity. We investigated the crypt. Mazarin found a concealed passage descending deeper.

A second pulse. A voice entered our minds:  
“You are out of position. This is not sanctioned.”

The tunnels gave way to a cavern, vast and oppressive. A third pulse.  
“Understand this. Each of you—your titles, your seal, your mandate—is you from my glorious mind. If I am fallible, then so are you. If I am compromised, then your appointment is an error.”

I suspected we were close to the Oracle itself. My thoughts stalled and I found myself speechless.

Elvire was waiting, but we did not receive the promised attack from our last confrontation. Instead: a negotiation. A dark mass of shadow writhed at his back, Mazarin tells us that this was only a projection, that its true form is deeper in the cavern.

The Oracle addressed Yedan as a woman named Yosuka, wearing Sixth House leather and all the trappings of a history I do not know. Then it appeared as Marrion, which unsettled me more. The Oracle has degraded. Sacrifice of dissidents alone could not sustain it; mindflayers were an attempted supplement. If the Oracle dies, the Machine fails. Its proposition: halt the investigation and prevent further inquiry. 

In exchange, by midday tomorrow, Elvire will surrender. He will claim sole responsibility for the aberrations. The mindflayer mutations will cease. The Scriptorum will meet its quotas. 

It claimed allegiance to the Founder’s vision. Claimed that our fates are intertwined. Claimed it had chosen us—for what, it would not say. Claimed that any citizen number, including ours, could be reclassified as dissident at will. A warning dressed as a blessing. A threat framed as favor. The Oracle speaks like a god and calculates like a machine.

Everything is shrouded in suggestion—what it wants, what it offers, what it threatens. Nothing is clear. I need clarity. I need truth. So I follow Mazarin and Charles deeper, because there is no safety, and certainly no perfection, in ignorance.
