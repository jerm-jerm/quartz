We regrouped the morning after the railway incident as the train was nearing the Sanctiforge. Marrion formally introduced herself as our handler. She is an arm of the High Regent, and by extension, serves the will of the Founder directly. Her demeanor is efficient, though she carries tension beneath the surface. She explained that she has been under scrutiny, particularly due to the Oracle’s unexpected deviation that led to our selection.

She briefed us on the situation. There is aberrant activity within the Sanctiforge, centered in the Shardlight District, which houses the bulk of the waged laborers. The district has missed its production quota for two quarters, due to worker shortages. Workers are going missing, others are found with apparent psychosis, or in the streets starved.

Marrion noted Lord Thassen Elvire as a potential instigator, he is proselytizing more than normal and has been the most adversarial to her and her position.

Our investigation began at the crematorium. We were chaperoned by District Bishop Theodore Clavers, a quirky and talkative scholar of the axiomatic order. We examined the body of Jeffery Solian, a 96-year-old Logistica worker, though his tissues were well-hydrated, he had clear signs of starvation. I noted residual magical depletion, specifically concentrated around the cerebral and vascular systems. Mazarin identified a lingering psychic imprint as well.

Solian’s widow was the elf from the train. She appeared at the crematorium and was clearly unsettled by our presence, though Mazarin managed to ease her concerns. She revealed that she had fallen ill and had received care in the Shardlight District. Whatever afflicted her, she believed, was the same thing that took her husband.  I attempted to offer comfort. I told her, in the most honest way I knew, that death is not as fearsome as most believe. But words have a way of failing me, emotion is a language I have forgotten. 

From there, we visited a local choir hall, hoping one of the missionaries might recognize Solian. The effort was poorly conceived and even more poorly executed. Charles lost patience with the director and struck him. We were promptly arrested. Marrion posted our bail and altered the reports at significant cost. I do not yet understand the others chosen by The Oracle. And yet, my name, my record, my house reputation now sits beside theirs. I have no control over their actions, and yet I am responsible for the outcome. It is a disquieting equation. 

I spoke with Marrion afterward. She tried to hide the tears at first, but they came all the same. Her anger followed soon after. She was overwhelmed and when I expressed how this assignment was the opposite of what I have been prepared for, she exploded. Her volatility reminded me of training, although at home, there is less emotion and more punishment. She told me my mother would not have faltered at such obstacles. I believe she is right. If I am to succeed here, I must do more than observe, I must lead. Perhaps I should not have let her speak to me in the manner she did. But I am no longer the heir. And she was right.

As we reconvened I discussed with Charles that he would need to act more reasonably going forward. It seemed as though Mazarin had already come to that conclusion and Charles agreed. We departed for the Shardlight Distict. 

NOTE: A halfling resembling Yedan Neric was reportedly seen "trading" without the owners knowledge a Sanctiforge charm for a valuable flask. 

There were invalids laying in the streets of the Shardlight District, visibly affected in the same way Jeffery Solian was. I asked Mazarin about a similar aura of arcane energy I noticed around her. She said it is likely due to her unusual type of magic. Her candor was refreshing.

Unexpectedly, we encountered Bishop Thassen Elvire in person. His presence is disarming, the aura around him mirrors both Mazarin’s and the affected worshippers.

Elvire claimed the faithful have strayed from the Founder’s true word, that leaders like the High Regent and the Master of Spycraft are corrupt, seeking only power. He believes he acts in service of the Founder’s will directly and was adamant that the Oracle was tampered with in our selection as a unit.

After he departed, on quite malevolent terms I will add, the vagrant man he had treated with the "Water of Life" transformed, his head splitting and reforming into a mindflayer.

It is clearer now that whatever this "training" mission was meant to be, it is in fact something else entirely, and I must become something else to meet it.
