The engagement with the [[Mindflayer Abberations|mindflayers]] was decisive, though inelegant. In my altered state, I fought alongside the others. The discipline of the dance was enhanced by something more raw, a force I could neither master nor repel. Necessary for victory, but invasive and costly. When it broke and my form returned to its mortal limits, a sickness rose, a physical rejection against the intrusion.

I was certain [[Yedan-Neric]] did not survive. I witnessed the frenzy overtake him, the destruction of the contents of his mind. I grabbed the necklace he requested I retrieve in his last moments. The portrait inside a beautiful woman, if he has family I will endeavor to return it. We gathered what evidence and supplies we could before making the return to Vellhum Heights.

Both [[Theodore Calvers|Theodore]] and [[Marrion Virelle|Marrion]] were visibly affected upon receiving Yedan’s remains. Theodore, lacking any knowledge of his burial preferences, conducted the rites in [[The Sanctiforge|Sanctiforge]] manner. Efficient, if impersonal. We ought to have established such details when first were assigned. For myself, as after every Pruning, my body, when its use is ended, should be burned without words or ceremony. At death, the body’s relevance ceases.

[[Charles Brand|Charles]] and I later checked on Marrion, who had withdrawn quickly following the rites. Her demeanor was marked by grief, her eyes reddened from tears. I informed her truthfully that our earlier omissions were to protect her from potential danger, and that she is valued. While speaking, I noticed a folder labeled _Permutational Dissidence_ on the floor near her desk and briefly pondered the meaning. Returning it produced no visible reaction, though her stoicism evoked the layered political maneuvering of the Great Game in [[Conscriptus]]. She extended an invitation for drinks. Charles declined, insisting the invitation was personal in nature. I disagreed.

En route, I encountered [[Mazarin]], who also suggested the possibility of a romantic overture. I dismissed this as irrelevant. Marrion’s position and connections warrant closer study; this meeting was an opportunity for observation. Still, I cannot entirely dismiss the quality of her presence and personality.

I will not espouse the evening in great detail. I learned enough to know that something had made Marrion angry and vengeful in the past and likely something happened to her parents. Yet when I inquired, relying on the hazy memory of our Greenbottle Reserve bond, she insisted both were alive and well. I also witnessed, whom I believe was High Regent Quaid, interrogating her on the corruption of [[The Oracle]] and the commencement of Block 252. She departed abruptly after this memory.

Today’s events represent a failure of internal discipline. For the first time, I feel the pull toward Pruning, to excise impulse and restore precision of purpose.

I control my body. No vestige of bloodline will command me.  
I control my mind. No distraction will fracture my focus.  
I control my actions. I do not permit touch to breach my guard as drilled into me since childhood. I have been Pruned before for such lapses in the heat of battle. To have allowed this unbidden closeness is an error without justification.
Perfection or death. There is no third path.
