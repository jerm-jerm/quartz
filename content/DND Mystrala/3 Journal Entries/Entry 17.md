The nesting grounds lay deep within the clay warrens. The animated dragon corpse rose from the rear of the formation. 

Freya, Mazarin, Enoch, and I engaged the frontlines while Sal and Charles used their skills from a distance. An avatar of death appeared, threatening Freya and House Maelthrumm before being felled by Mazarin.

A gilded playing card rested in his breast pocket. A King of Clubs, from an ornate deck, with the word “Death” inscribed beneath. Sal noted the insignia of an institution on the avatar’s attire: the Magistraium, also known as Aetherforge College, and specifically the High Etherium Conservatory. Highly selective but not known specifically for producing necromancers.

Enoch uncovered additional abnormalities inside the dragon’s remains: charms embedded with deliberate symmetry. A type of arcane battery meant to sustain the corpse and enhance its abilities. 

We constructed a pyre for Freya’s fallen bulettes. She spoke a few words, a Maelthrumm specific prayer. 

Freya suspects the necromancer has allied with the Fifth Recruits, an insurrectionist group of naval deserters, Steel Covenant defectors, and former Switchrail wardens, numbering nearly a thousand. No missives have passed to engage them, thus they remain undisturbed in the southern area of [[Conscriptus]].

We returned to the Katharis estate without further interruption.

Charles withdrew to his quarters. **NOTE:** Staff accounts indicate that he spent time writing to Belladonna Vorynth and studying the weapon she provided him. External observers described the weapon as expressing “hunger” and “restlessness,” and noted that Charles appeared physically impacted by its bond.

I navigated to Marrion’s quarters. The aftermath of the ride and the hunt still carried a kind of momentum and it seemed an appropriate moment to finish what we began days ago.

I asked whether she, or more precisely the Spymaster, intended to use me and our cohort. Her reaction was controlled but unmistakably sharp, something nearer to tightly masked frustration than simple discontent. She reminded me of the risks she has taken on our behalf and the personal cost of doing so. She referred to my position as _“naval royalty",_ a title I detest, and her meaning was plain enough: I am too deeply entwined in this to be anything but an asset she must handle carefully.

I sought clarity: whether she saw me as a person or merely as a convenient instrument.

Rather than answer, she stepped in to kiss me, closing the distance in place of an explanation. I withdrew, waiting for something intentional rather than impulsive. When she finally spoke, she offered only that she and many others had a vested interest in my success.

I left before I could say much more, only that many seemed invested in my success, and how I hoped she might be invested in _me_. At the door, I saw the start of tears. I returned, briefly, and kissed her. Not forgiveness. Only acknowledgment.

I like her, and I believe she feels something in return. But her position requires caution, and I am shaped as I am. Understanding the reasons does little to soften the ache.
