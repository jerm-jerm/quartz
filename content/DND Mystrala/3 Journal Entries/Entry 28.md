Reports indicate he performed adequately, adapting to conditions rather than attempting to dominate them outright. This is… characteristic.

The Twilight Gala functioned as both intermission and evaluation. Appearances were maintained, alliances tested through implication rather than declaration. We were approached by Jack Donahugh, a representative of the Silverstream family, who expressed interest in endorsing our group based on observed performance. His tone suggested opportunity. His timing suggested calculation.

Engagements with House Valantis and other figures of note followed. Each interaction carried subtext—questions unasked, answers implied. Conscriptus does not deal in sincerity. It deals in positioning.

The evening deteriorated, predictably, into conflict.

Freya initiated hostilities under the guise of insult. The altercation escalated into a full engagement within the gala space. I did not hesitate. The exchange was brief, contained, and decisive. In its wake, I was confronted by a representative of House Maelthrumm. What followed was not negotiation, but trial.

The duel was unexpected. It was also necessary.

Her technique was refined, though rigid in places. I adjusted accordingly. The outcome was not absolute, but it was sufficient. The blessing granted thereafter was not symbolic—it carried weight, both arcane and political. I accepted it without acknowledgment beyond what was required. To do otherwise would have diminished its value.

**NOTE:** Enoch reports intercepting Belladonna Vorynth prior to her reaching Charles. During this interaction, she demonstrated awareness of Luminora Katharis’ identity and stated objectives. Her message was direct: cease current actions. The delivery was not framed as a threat, but as a warning. The distinction is negligible. Her knowledge indicates either exceptional intelligence gathering—or proximity.

This development is… suboptimal.

We have proceeded regardless.

Mazarin’s challenge approaches, followed by the final duels. Preparation has shifted accordingly. There is less room now for error, less tolerance for deviation.

If Belladonna is aware, then others may be as well. That does not alter the objective. It refines the margin within which it must be executed.

Dominance, here, is not declared. It is demonstrated.

I will ensure that demonstration is sufficient.
