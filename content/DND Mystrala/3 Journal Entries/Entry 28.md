Charles entered the sharpshooter challenge and achieved victory. His precision under pressure remains consistent, though the source of that consistency continues to warrant scrutiny. From external observation and his own accounts there was outside interference to keep him from succeeding.

Enoch encountered Kessaria prior to his event, who provided proprietary advice regarding his event. The remainder of the nature of their interaction remains unclear, though his composure shifted in its aftermath. His challenge, hosted by House Vorynth, consisted of an obstacle course integrating summoned entities, mechanical traps, and direct competition with other participants. He performed more than adequately, adapting to conditions rather than attempting to dominate them outright. Which has become characteristic for his manner.

The Twilight Gala functioned as both intermission and evaluation. Appearances were maintained, alliances tested through implication rather than declaration. We were approached by Jack Donahugh, a representative of the Silverstream family, a weapons merchant, who expressed interest in endorsing our group based on observed performance.

Engagements with House Valantis and other figures of note followed. Each interaction carried subtext: questions unasked, answers implied. Conscriptus deals in positioning. The evening deteriorated, somewhat predictably, into conflict.

Freya initiated hostilities, the altercation escalated into a full duel within the gala space. I did not hesitate. The exchange was brief, contained, and decisive.

Her technique was aggressive, though refined in places. I adjusted accordingly. The outcome was sufficient. The blessing granted thereafter carried weight. I accepted it without acknowledgment, to do otherwise would have diminished its value.

**NOTE:** Enoch reports intercepting Belladonna Vorynth prior to her reaching Charles. During this interaction, she demonstrated awareness of Luminora Katharis’ identity and stated objectives. Her message was direct: cease current actions. The delivery was not framed as a threat, but as a warning  Her knowledge indicates either exceptional intelligence gathering or unknown proximity to the group.

This development is suboptimal but we proceeded regardless.

Mazarin’s challenge approaches, followed by the final duels. Preparation has shifted accordingly. There is less room now for error, less tolerance for deviation.

If Belladonna is aware, then others may be as well. That does not alter the objective.
