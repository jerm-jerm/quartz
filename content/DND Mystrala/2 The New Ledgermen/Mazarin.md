## Impression
Socially astute, she reads the undercurrents of a room faster than most. When outcomes deviate from her intention, she does not mask her frustration. It’s immediate, visible. A stark contrast to her otherwise measured temperament.

Despite this, she largely maintains a calm, composed presence. Her past is occluded. Even within the Trials, her records were limited or sealed. That level of classification is rare.

## Known Record
Previously affiliated with the [[The Sixth House]], though her exact role is unstated. She wields a psionic variant of arcana. The energy profile resembles those of [[Thassen Elvire]] and the [[Mindflayer Abberations]]. She demonstrates stable control and a strong innate talent for mental manipulation and perception.
