## Impression
Sal Brentwood is an older man with no place on a battlefield. Age and frailty alone might make him ill-fitted for combat, but more than that, his habits make him profoundly unsafe to stand beside. He is volatile in the literal sense, carrying alchemical components and devices on his person without warning. I have never encountered an individual quite like him, and I cannot imagine the calamity of an army composed of his likeness.

## Known Record
During our commencement hearing, it was revealed that his niece, Lessa Brentwood, was listed as a dissident and reallocated. With what we now know of [[The Oracle|The Oracle's]] consumption of minds, one can presume the worst.

Sal has taken it upon himself to support Lessa’s two children. Tomas was enrolled in the juvenile military academy in [[Conscriptus]], while Lilian was sent to an orphanage. Sal diverts what money he can to their care.

Back home, he operated a business, _Sal’s Salves_, which he insists was the finest alchemical storefront in existence. In truth, his storefront was unsanctioned and unlicensed, thus illegal. He nevertheless seems proud of it, and speaks of it often.

## NOTE (Editorial Addenda)
Records indicate Sal Brentwood was expelled from Arc Light University, likely due to an intentional alchemical incident, though the details remain unclear. Additionally, he has a history of delinquency in taxes, and his business was flagged for multiple other violations prior to its closure.
