Session Four: Battle with the Forgemaster

The group pressed deeper into the forge, a massive seam tore open in the sky, flooding the area with crimson light. The disturbance caused significant destruction, prompting some within the forge to flee while others barricaded themselves inside.

Amid the confusion, the party continued its search. Evidence suggested acts of cannibalism among the desperate workers. They managed to rescue several trapped individuals before discovering a concealed doorway leading to the Forgemaster’s office, richly adorned and clearly meant for someone of authority.

Corvin seized the Forgemaster without warning, assaulting him before anyone could intervene. Though a confrontation seemed inevitable, Corvin acted with surprising speed. The pursuit led through various corridors until both combatants crashed through a glass window. The fall proved devastating for the Forgemaster, and Corvin delivered the final blow before recovering a potion and a radiant sun pendant from the body.
