Session Five: The Rift Inside Cindralis

The group investigated the rift within the Forge of Chains, an unstable tear leading directly to the Elemental Plane of Fire. While the city’s forges have long drawn upon the plane’s heat, this connection was far more volatile and uncontrolled.

The group recovered demolition and forge tools, seemingly crafted from a special metal. Corven carried the tools as the group continued deeper through the partially abandoned complex, encountering scattered resistance from those who remained behind. Before leaving, they used the symbol taken from the Forgemaster, a sigil that opened two sealed doors. Inside, they discovered an ornate branding iron etched with Ignus sigils and an amulet radiating a steady, unnatural heat.

Exiting the forge, they found the city in open chaos. Fighting had broken out between the Emberbrands and various local factions, spreading destruction far beyond the foundry. Despite the turmoil, the group successfully returned to Silvara, bringing with them six barrels of explosives and other valuable materials.

Silvara was found gravely injured, her left arm mangled beyond healing. Plio tended to her wounds, restoring what she could through divine means.
