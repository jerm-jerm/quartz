Session Two: Refugee Escape

A new figure joined the group, a man named Joe. Plio enjoyed his upbeat, quirky energy. He claimed to have come from the forest, which left Corvin openly baffled. Joe mentioned Karsus as his place of origin, unusual, as few, if any, are known to make the journey from into the FETC.

The group gathered the refugees and began leading them out. Along the way, Plio walked among them, offering healing, dressing wounds, and speaking words of encouragement. Corvin observed her efforts and told her she could do more than mend scrapes and that she needed to think on a larger scale.

The escape was interrupted by a group of Cindralis enforcers. There was no time to explain or talk the situation down; battle was unavoidable. Corvin engaged the captain directly and seemed to take a kind of grim enjoyment in throwing himself into the fight, even as he absorbed several heavy blows.

One of the refugees was struck during the clash, though quick healing kept them alive. Despite the danger, all refugees survived and were successfully led out. The group later rejoined Silvara and Dorn, who appeared to have had a far easier time completing her part of the effort.
